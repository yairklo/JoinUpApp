JoinUp logo usage guide

The symbol is a ring with one slot cut out and one dot stepping in: “one more player”. Hebrew-first UI, Latin wordmark.

Lockup and symbol

Use the lockup by default. The symbol alone is for avatars, favicons and app icons. Do not recreate or retype the wordmark: its letters are custom outlines.

Colour

Brand green #047857 · RGB 4 120 87 · on white 5.48:1
Accent orange #D9480F (the dot only) · on white 4.30:1
Dark-theme green #10B981 · on #0b1220 7.38:1
Dark-theme orange #F2672A · on #0b1220 6.02:1

#047857 is only 3.41:1 on #0b1220, so on dark surfaces always use the on-dark files. CMYK/Pantone are not specified: match by eye from a proof.

Approved backgrounds

White, #0b1220 (on-dark), brand green (white version), and single-colour black for print/fax/stamps. Photos: only where the area behind the logo is quiet and reaches 3:1.

Clear space and minimum size

  • Clear space = the symbol’s diameter ÷ 5 on all sides (dashed area).
  • Symbol: min 16 px / 5 mm.
  • Lockup: min 96 px wide / 25 mm. Wordmark only: 64 px.

Typography and RTL

  • UI and Hebrew text: Heebo (Regular 400 / SemiBold 600). The rounded monoline wordmark is a custom drawing that pairs with it.
  • The logo is Latin and is never mirrored in RTL layouts: symbol left, wordmark right, in both directions.
  • In Hebrew copy write “JoinUp” in Latin letters; keep it the same size as surrounding text, don’t restyle it.

App icons

  • Android adaptive: white mark, solid #047857 background, mark inside the 66 dp safe zone.
  • Play Store: play-store-icon-512.png, full bleed, no rounded corners (Google masks it).
  • Web: favicon.svg auto-switches to the dark colours; favicon.ico as fallback.

Don’t

Rotate or tilt
Stretch or squash
Light-bg green on dark
On busy / low-contrast fills
Shadows, glows, gradients

Also: don’t move or recolour the dot (except to one-colour), don’t fill the slot, don’t add a sport graphic, don’t set the wordmark in another font, don’t place the orange dot on green (use the white version).
